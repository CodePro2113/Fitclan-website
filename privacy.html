01 Who We Are

FitClan ("we", "us", "our") is an independent mobile application and website operated by Nishant Mittal. FitClan is a free family fitness tracking and gamification app available on Android via the Google Play Store.

This Privacy Policy applies to:

  • The FitClan Android application
  • The FitClan website at fitclan.ca (and any subdomains)
  • Any related services, notifications, or communications from FitClan

By using FitClan, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please discontinue use of the app and website.

02 Data We Collect

We collect the minimum data necessary to provide the FitClan service. Here is a complete breakdown:

Category Data Collected Source Required?
Account Email address, display name, optional profile photo You provide directly Yes
Fitness Data Step count, calories burned, distance walked/run, active minutes Android Health Connect Yes
Activity Logs Manually logged activities (type, duration, date) You provide directly Optional

What we do NOT collect: We do not collect payment information, government IDs, social security numbers, precise GPS location, call logs, contacts, or any data beyond what is listed above.

03 How We Use Your Data

All data we collect is used exclusively to provide and improve the FitClan service. Specifically:

  • Account data — to authenticate you, identify you within your family group, and personalize your experience
  • Fitness data — to calculate your daily and weekly points, populate the leaderboard, and track streaks and milestones
  • Family group data — to show the family leaderboard, weekly goals, and member rankings to all family members in your group
  • Push tokens — to send you streak reminders, leaderboard change alerts, mission nudges, and weekly recap notifications (only if you grant notification permission)
  • Device info — to diagnose crashes and ensure compatibility
  • Anonymous usage data — to understand which features are used and improve the app

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or sale to third parties — ever.

04 Health & Fitness Data (Android Health Connect)

FitClan requests access to Android Health Connect to read fitness data including steps, calories, distance, and active minutes. This is the core function of the app.

What We Access

  • Steps (read only)
  • Calories burned (read only)
  • Distance (read only)
  • Active minutes / exercise sessions (read only)

How We Use It

Health Connect data is read solely to calculate your FitClan point score and populate your personal dashboard and family leaderboard. We do not write data back to Health Connect.

Health Connect Data Policy: Health Connect data is never used for advertising, never shared with third-party analytics platforms, never sold, and never used for any purpose outside of providing the FitClan leaderboard service. This data is stored securely in your FitClan account on Supabase servers and is deleted when you delete your account.

Revoking Access

You can revoke FitClan's access to Health Connect at any time through Android Settings → Health Connect → App permissions → FitClan. Revoking access will prevent new fitness data from syncing but will not delete previously synced data from your FitClan account (you can do that via account deletion).

05 Children's Privacy

FitClan is designed for use by families, which may include children under the age of 13. We take children's privacy seriously.

Account Creation

FitClan requires users to be at least 13 years old to create their own account. Children under 13 may use FitClan only as part of a family group where a parent or legal guardian has created the family account and manages membership.

Parental Consent

By adding a child under 13 to a FitClan family group, the parent or guardian represents that they have consented to the collection and use of the child's fitness data as described in this policy, solely for the purpose of participating in the family leaderboard.

Data Collected for Children

For child members of a family group (under 13), we collect only:

  • A display name (set by the parent — does not need to be the child's real name)
  • Fitness data from Health Connect (steps, calories, distance) on the child's device, if the parent installs and configures the app on that device
  • Points and leaderboard position within the family group

We do not collect email addresses, phone numbers, or any other identifying information from child users.

Parental Rights

Parents and guardians may at any time: review their child's data, request deletion of their child's data, or remove their child from the family group. Contact us at privacy@fitclan.ca to exercise these rights.

COPPA: If you are a parent in the United States and believe we have inadvertently collected personal information from your child under 13 without consent, please contact us immediately at privacy@fitclan.ca and we will promptly delete that data.

07 Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your FitFam account is active. Specifically:

  • Account data: Retained until you delete your account
  • Fitness data: Retained for your activity history until account deletion
  • Push tokens: Deleted when you revoke notification permissions or delete your account
  • Anonymous usage data: May be retained indefinitely in aggregate/anonymized form

Account Deletion

You can delete your FitClan account at any time from within the app (Settings → Account → Delete Account) or by emailing privacy@fitclan.ca. Upon deletion, all personal data including your account details, fitness history, and family group memberships will be permanently deleted within 30 days.

Note: if you are the owner of a family group, deleting your account will also delete the family group and remove all members. We recommend transferring ownership before deleting your account.

08 Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your data:

  • All data is transmitted over encrypted HTTPS connections
  • Data is stored in Supabase with row-level security policies ensuring users can only access their own data and their family group's data
  • Authentication uses Supabase Auth with industry-standard JWT tokens
  • Health Connect data is accessed on-device and transmitted only over encrypted connections to our servers
  • We do not store passwords in plain text — authentication is managed by Supabase Auth

No method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure. While we strive to protect your data, we cannot guarantee absolute security. If you discover a security vulnerability, please responsibly disclose it to security@fitclan.ca.

09 Your Rights

You have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Access: You can request a copy of all data we hold about you
  • Correction: You can update your display name and profile information in the app at any time
  • Deletion: You can delete your account and all associated data (see Section 7)
  • Portability: You can request an export of your fitness history and account data in a machine-readable format
  • Objection: You can opt out of push notifications at any time via your device's notification settings
  • Health Connect: You can revoke Health Connect permissions at any time (see Section 4)

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at privacy@fitclan.ca. We will respond within 30 days.

If you are in the European Economic Area (EEA) or United Kingdom, you also have rights under GDPR/UK GDPR, including the right to lodge a complaint with your local supervisory authority.
