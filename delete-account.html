Account Deletion

Delete your FitClan
account & data

You have the right to delete your account at any time. You can do it yourself in seconds — in the app or on the web — or email us and we'll handle it. Either way, deletion is immediate and permanent.

Option 1 — In the FitClan app

1

Open the app & go to Profile

Make sure you're signed in to the account you want to delete, then open the Profile tab.

2

Scroll down & tap "Delete Account"

You'll find it below the Sign Out button. Tap it to open the confirmation dialog.

3

Type DELETE to confirm

Type DELETE and press Permanently Delete My Account. Your account and all data are removed immediately.

Option 2 — On the web

1

Sign in to the web app

Go to app.fitclan.ca and sign in with the account you want to delete.

2

Open Profile → Delete Account

From your Profile page, scroll to the bottom and select Delete Account.

3

Type DELETE to confirm

Confirm by typing DELETE. Your account and all associated data are removed immediately.

What data is deleted vs. retained

Data type
On deletion
Profile (name, avatar, email)
Permanently deleted immediately
Activity logs & points history
Permanently deleted immediately

Permanently deleted immediately

Option 3 — Can't sign in?

If you've lost access to your account, email us directly. Include the email address associated with your FitClan account and we'll delete it within 7 business days.
