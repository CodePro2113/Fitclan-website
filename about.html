About FitClan

Built for families
who move together

FitClan is a free Android app that turns everyday movement into a live family leaderboard. Steps, workouts, and streaks — all competing, all together.

The story

FitClan started with a simple observation: fitness apps are built for individuals, but movement is more fun when it's a family sport. Step counters and calorie trackers are great solo tools, but they don't capture the magic of a family where everyone is watching the leaderboard and texting each other "you're going down" at 9pm.

The idea was straightforward — take your family's Health Connect data, award points for every walk, run, swim, and bike ride, and put everyone on a live leaderboard. Make it feel like a game rather than a chore. Add Conquest Mode so you can literally fight over Russia with your step count.

FitClan is built in Canada, entirely by one developer, and is free forever. No ads, no subscriptions, no data selling. Just your family moving more.

Our Mission

Make movement a daily family habit, not a solo obligation.

The research is clear — people move more when they're accountable to others. FitClan uses that psychology to turn the whole family into an accountability group, automatically. Your kids see your step count. You see theirs. Everyone pushes a little harder because the leaderboard is live and the family chat is unforgiving.

We believe fitness should be social, accessible, and free. Not a premium subscription, not a gym membership, not another thing you have to remember to log manually. Just move, and the app does the rest.

What we stand for
🔒

Privacy First

Your health data is yours. We never sell it, never show ads, and never share it with anyone outside the services needed to run the app.

🆓

Free Forever

Core FitClan features will always be free. We may add optional extras in the future, but we won't gate what already exists behind a paywall.

👨‍👩‍👧

Family Safe

Designed for families including kids. No social feed, no strangers, no public profiles — your leaderboard is only visible to your family group.

⚡

Zero Friction

Steps sync automatically from Health Connect. No manual logging, no remembering to open the app. It just works in the background.

🎮

Actually Fun

Leaderboards, Conquest Mode, missions, streaks, and weekly crowns. Fitness shouldn't feel like work — it should feel like a game.

🚀

Always Improving

FitClan ships updates regularly based on user feedback. iOS, Apple Health, Samsung Health, and new game modes are all on the roadmap.

The app is built with React Native (Capacitor), TypeScript, Supabase, and Android Health Connect. Every feature you see was shipped by one person who also has to answer support emails.
